Minimalist Master Suite

Location
Madhapur, Hyderabad
Project Type
Master Bedroom & Walk-in Closet
Completion
2026

Project Overview

Designed as a tranquil sanctuary away from the city's pulse, this master suite combines soft oat upholstery, warm wood tones, fluted glass wardrobe panels, and indirect coves.

Project Challenge & Solution

Challenge:

Creating a serene atmosphere while accommodating substantial storage requirements.

Parthu Interiors Solution:

We designed a hidden walk-in closet behind a seamless fluted glass partition wall, keeping clothes out of sight from the main sleeping area.
