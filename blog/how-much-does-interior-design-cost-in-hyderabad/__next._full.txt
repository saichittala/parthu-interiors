<h2 id="key-factors-that-impact-your-final-quote">Key Factors That Impact Your Final Quote</h2>
<h3 id="1-substrate-material-plywood-grade">1. Substrate Material (Plywood Grade)</h3>
<p>Never compromise on plywood in Hyderabad's climate. Commercial MR (Moisture Resistant) plywood is suitable for dry bedroom wardrobes, but <strong>100% BWP (Boiling Water Proof) Marine Plywood</strong> (IS 710 certified) is mandatory for kitchens, bathrooms, and utility zones to prevent water damage and termite infestation.</p>
<h3 id="2-shutter-finishes">2. Shutter Finishes</h3>
<ul><li><strong>Laminate (1mm):</strong> Budget-friendly, highly durable, available in matt and gloss.</li><li><strong>Acrylic (2mm):</strong> High-gloss glass-like reflection, scratch-resistant, anti-fingerprint.</li><li><strong>Natural Veneer:</strong> Authentic wood grain warmth, requires PU clear polish coating.</li><li><strong>PU Lacquer Paint:</strong> Seamless luxury finish with zero edge lines.</li></ul>
<h3 id="3-hardware-brands">3. Hardware Brands</h3>
<p>Using genuine German fittings from <strong>Blum, Hafele, or Hettich</strong> adds 15-20% to hardware cost but guarantees smooth soft-close drawer operation for 10-15 years without sagging.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-avoid-hidden-costs">How to Avoid Hidden Costs</h2>
<p>1.  <strong>Demand an Itemized BOQ:</strong> Ensure your quote explicitly lists plywood brand, sheet thickness, laminate code, hardware model, and ceiling material.</p>
<p>2.  <strong>Verify Factory Edge-Banding:</strong> Hand-glued edge banding peels off within 2 years. Factory-applied PUR hotmelt edge banding provides a 100% waterproof seal.</p>
<p>3.  <strong>Check Turnkey Scope:</strong> Confirm if electrical alterations, magnetic track lights, ceiling paint, quartz installation, and post-site deep cleaning are included.</p>
