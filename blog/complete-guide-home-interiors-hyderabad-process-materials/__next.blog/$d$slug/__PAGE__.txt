<p>Receiving the keys to your new 2BHK, 3BHK, or villa in Hyderabad is a major moment. But turning a vacant concrete structure into a comfortable, functional, and aesthetically pleasing home requires systematic planning.</p>
<p>This guide outlines the complete step-by-step interior execution process used by <strong>Parthu Interiors</strong>.</p>
<p>---</p>
<h2 id="phase-1-spatial-discovery-amp-floorplan-assessment">Phase 1: Spatial Discovery &amp; Floorplan Assessment</h2>
<ul><li><strong>On-Site Measurement:</strong> Precise laser measurement of walls, ceiling heights, door clearances, and electrical junction boxes.</li><li><strong>Lifestyle Profiling:</strong> Understanding your daily routines, storage needs, family size, cooking habits, and aesthetic preferences.</li></ul>
<h2 id="phase-2-2d-spatial-layouts-amp-3d-visualization">Phase 2: 2D Spatial Layouts &amp; 3D Visualization</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Ergonomic Layouts:</strong> Defining optimal placements for furniture, walk-in closets, kitchen work triangles, and TV viewing angles.</li><li><strong>Photorealistic 3D Renders:</strong> Previewing exact color combinations, lighting scenes, and material textures before any wood is cut.</li></ul>
<h2 id="phase-3-material-selection-amp-frozen-boq">Phase 3: Material Selection &amp; Frozen BOQ</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Material Moodboard:</strong> Hand-selecting marine plywood grades, acrylic laminates, quartz countertops, glass panels, and hardware.</li><li><strong>Itemized Budget Locking:</strong> Signing a frozen Bill of Quantities (BOQ) to ensure zero price escalations during execution.</li></ul>
<h2 id="phase-4-factory-fabrication-kokapet-plant">Phase 4: Factory Fabrication (Kokapet Plant)</h2>
